 How do I sign your Data Processing Addendum?

 How do I sign your Data Processing Addendum?

 September 16, 2026 

 Table of contents

 Issue

 I need to sign a DPA with Sentry.

 Applies To

 All Plans

 Resolution

 If you prefer a signed copy of our DPA, go to this DocuSign link and follow the prompts.
​
We offer a Data Processing Addendum for customers who send personal data to Sentry. You’ll find our DPA in the Legal & Compliance section of your organization’s navigation menu in Sentry. It can be accessed by any member of the Sentry organization, but can only be signed/accepted by members with an Owner or Billing role.

 Note: All members can view this document, but only Billing or Owner members can sign or accept it.

 If you’d like to know who signed it, you can check your organization’s audit log. 

 You can also reach out to Support for assistance.
​
Changes to the DPA are only available to Enterprise customers .
