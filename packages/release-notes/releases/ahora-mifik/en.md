## Changes

- **New name: mifik.** We used to be called fik. The name now matches the address (mifik.cl), so
  we're easier to find. Your account, your data and the way you sign in don't change.
- **The logo reads mifik**, with the same hand-drawn mountain range.
- **The home page opens directly at mifik.cl**, without going through another address. If you had
  mifik.cl/es/ saved, it takes you to the same place.
- **Import templates you already downloaded keep working**: the column for existing data is now
  called "ID mifik", and "ID fik" is still read.
- In your authenticator app or your passkeys, what you already registered may still show as "fik".
  It keeps working the same.
