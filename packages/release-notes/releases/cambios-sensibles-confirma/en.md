---

## Security

- **Removing a passkey asks you to confirm it's you**: just like adding one. Removing it also
  closes the other sessions opened with that passkey; yours stays open.
- **Changing your RUT asks too**: it's how you sign in and recover your password, so now you confirm
  before changing it, we let you know by email, and it can no longer be left empty.
- **Fairer sign-in locks**: when a lock for wrong passwords ends, the account starts from zero, so
  nobody can keep it locked with an attempt every now and then.
- **Settings that don't undo your security**: saving your profile or preferences no longer reverts a
  security change made at the same time on another device.
