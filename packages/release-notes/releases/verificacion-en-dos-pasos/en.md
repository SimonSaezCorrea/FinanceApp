---
title: Easier two-step verification
summary: A code by email, remember this device and new recovery codes.
---

## New

- **Code by email**: besides the authenticator app, you can get the two-step verification code at
  your confirmed email. It expires after 10 minutes and you can ask for another from the same screen.
- **Remember this device**: when you type the code you can choose not to be asked again in that
  browser for 30 days. Your password is always still required. In Profile → Security you see the
  remembered devices and can remove any of them.
- **Generate new recovery codes** without turning verification off: confirm it's you and get 10 new
  codes; the old ones stop working.
- **Switch method** between the app and email from Profile → Security, keeping your recovery codes.

## Changes

- Changing or recovering your password, changing or turning off verification, or generating new
  codes forgets every remembered device.
- We email you when you remember a device, generate new codes or switch method.
