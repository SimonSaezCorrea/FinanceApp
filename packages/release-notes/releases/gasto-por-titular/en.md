## New

- **The month's spending by category, account or cardholder**: on the Dashboard, "Where it went" has
  a switch to see the month's spending by category, by the account it was charged to or by who spent
  it. "Cardholder" shows up when someone you gave an additional card spent with it that month.
- **Cardholder on the movement**: the detail of a purchase made with an additional card shows whose
  name it's in.

## Changes

- **Your cards look more like the real ones**: they show their network (Visa, Mastercard, American
  Express or Redcompra), and an additional card carries its cardholder's name instead of yours.
