title: Adding a passkey or turning on two-step verification asks you to confirm it's you
summary: Before adding a new way into your account, you confirm it's you.
---

## Security

- **Adding a passkey asks you to confirm it's you**: before a new passkey is registered, you
  confirm with your code, another passkey, or your password if you have neither yet.
- **Turning on two-step verification asks too**: as when changing the method, now also when
  turning it on for the first time. So someone with your session open can't add a way in that they
  control.
