## New

- **Personal finance guides for Chile**, free and with no account: credit card closing and payment
  due dates, additional cards and a shared limit, credit line or credit card, dollar purchases on a
  credit card, and interest-free instalments. Each one cites its official sources (CMF, SERNAC,
  Diario Oficial) and explains the minimum payment under the CMF rule (NCG 537).
- The guides are signed by their author, the founder of mifik, and a new page explains
  [how we write them](https://mifik.cl/en/how-we-write/): where the facts come from, how often they
  are reviewed and how to report a mistake.
- Resources for AI assistants: a site summary at `/llms.txt`, a page that shows it, and on every
  guide the buttons to copy it or view it as Markdown.
- When you create a checking or demand account you can now set its credit line (overdraft) right
  away, instead of editing it afterwards.

## Improvements

- The site's footer is organized in columns: Indicators, Learn (the newest guides) and mifik.
- The home page is titled "mifik · Finanzas personales en Chile", and the site describes mifik better to
  search engines (name, contact, logo and founder).

## Fixes

- The note about adding a limit in another currency pointed to a profile section that no longer
  exists; it now points to Profile, Preferences, Currencies and amounts.
- The app's technical address (the API) no longer shows up in search engines.
