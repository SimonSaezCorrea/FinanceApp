## Security

- **Turning it off asks you to confirm it's you**: besides your password, turning off two-step
  verification asks for your code (or your passkey). So someone who knows your password and has your
  session open can't turn it off to set up their own.
- **Remember this device, better protected**: the mark that remembers your device is now explicitly
  declared as readable only by the server and only over a secure connection.
