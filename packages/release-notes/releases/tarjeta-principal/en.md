---
title: Change the primary card, credit per currency
summary: When your bank replaces the card, you pick the new primary in one step.
---

## New

- **Change the primary card**: when you deactivate or delete a credit card's primary, you choose in
  the same step which card becomes primary: another card on the account or the new one your bank
  sent. The account's credit doesn't change.
- **Make primary**: from an additional card's detail you can make it the primary whenever you want.
- If an account was left without an active primary card, a notice lets you choose one.

## Changes

- **Dollar credit belongs to the account**: every card spends from the same credit in each currency.
  The primary uses 100% of it and an additional card can have its own cap, always within the
  account's credit. Credit in other currencies is edited from the account.
- A purchase in a currency the account has no credit in is no longer accepted, and a dollar purchase
  never uses the pesos credit.

## Fixes

- Deactivated cards no longer show up when recording a movement, a template, an instalment purchase
  or a fixed expense. What was already recorded with them stays as it was.
