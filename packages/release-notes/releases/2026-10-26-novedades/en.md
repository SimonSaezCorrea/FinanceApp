## New

- **What's new**: a page that tells what changed in fik with each update, at
  [mifik.cl/en/whats-new](https://mifik.cl/en/whats-new/) and inside the app.
- In the app, a dot next to What's new tells you when there is something you haven't seen yet.
  Opening it clears the dot on all your devices.
