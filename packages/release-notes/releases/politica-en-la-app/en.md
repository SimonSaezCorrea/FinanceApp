## New

- **Privacy policy inside the app**: open it from Profile → Data and privacy without going to the
  public site. It's the same text published on mifik.cl.
- **Go to mifik.cl without signing out**: "fik website", under What's new (on your phone, in
  "More"), opens the public site in a new tab and you stay signed in.

## Changes

- **The logo takes you to the Dashboard**: tap the _fik_ logo from any section to go back to the
  Dashboard.
