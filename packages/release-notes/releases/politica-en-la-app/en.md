## New

- **Privacy policy inside the app**: open it from Profile → Data and privacy without going to the
  public site. It's the same text published on mifik.cl.
- **Leave without signing out**: the "Leave" button now asks what you want to do: go to the public
  site in a new tab, still signed in, or sign out.

## Changes

- **The logo takes you to the Dashboard**: tap the _mifik_ logo from any section to go back to the
  Dashboard.
- **What's coming, where it belongs**: in Profile, Notifications shows as "Coming soon" inside the App
  group, instead of a loose list at the foot of the index.
