## New

- **Templates**: save a movement you repeat often, like "Fuel" or "Rent", with its account,
  card, category and the rest of its details. The amount is optional and the date is always the
  day you use it.
- In **Movements**, the arrow on "New movement" lists your templates, and a shortcuts row above
  the table keeps them one tap away. Picking one opens the form already filled, so you only
  confirm.
- From an account's detail, the template records on that account.
- Create a template from the **Templates** section or with "Save as template" while recording a
  movement. While you create it, a preview shows how the movement will look.
