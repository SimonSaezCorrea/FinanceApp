title: Typing your password inside the app has an attempt limit too
summary: Attempts with your password inside the app count just like signing in.
---

## Security

- **An attempt limit inside the app**: when you confirm it's you with your password, change it,
  turn off two-step verification or delete your account, each wrong password counts just like
  signing in. After 5 in a row, you wait 15 minutes.
- **Security changes that don't overwrite each other**: changing your password or turning
  two-step verification on or off no longer undoes another security change made at the same time.
