---
title: Import by account
summary: Each account in Import has its own template, with its data, besides the bank statement.
---

## New

- **One account's template**: in Import, tap an account and choose what to do. Download its template
  empty or with its movements, transfers and statements, upload it once filled, or import your
  bank's statement as before.

## Changes

- **No accidental replacing**: a template with one account's data only adds new rows. "Replace
  everything" isn't available with it, so it never deletes your other accounts.
