title: Turning on two-step verification can only be finished where you started it
summary: A half-done setup can no longer be completed from another session, and codes have a limit.
---

## Security

- **Only from your session**: once you start turning on two-step verification, it can only be
  finished from the same session and within 30 minutes. After that, you start again.
- **A code limit while turning it on**: after 5 wrong codes in a row, you wait 15 minutes before
  trying again.
- **Others can't lock your sign-in**: wrong codes when confirming it's you inside the app count
  separately and no longer lock your two-step sign-in.
- **No shortcuts in parallel**: several codes sent at the same time all count, so the limit of 5
  always holds.
- **Templates of a sensible size**: an import template can create up to 20,000 instalments in
  total across all its plans.
