---
title: We catch errors sooner
summary: If something breaks in mifik, we get an alert to fix it, without your personal data.
---

## Improvements

- When something fails in the app or on the server, we get an alert with what we need to fix
  it, even if you don't write to us.

## Security

- Those alerts and the server's logs never carry your name, email, password or amounts. The
  providers that keep them (Sentry and Better Stack) are listed in the
  [privacy policy](https://mifik.cl/en/privacy-policy/).
