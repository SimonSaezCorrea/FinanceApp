## Improvements

- **A title per version**: in What's new, inside the app and on
  [mifik.cl/en/whats-new](https://mifik.cl/en/whats-new/), each version opens with a sentence
  summing up what it brought. The version number stays as a label above it.
- **In the list too**: on wide screens, the list of versions shows each one's title under its
  number.
