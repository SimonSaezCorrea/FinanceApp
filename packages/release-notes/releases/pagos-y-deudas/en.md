---
title: Card payments show on the card's account, and debts without a due date
summary: Paying a statement now also shows on the credit card's account, and a debt no longer needs a due date.
---

## Changes

- **A statement payment shows on the card's account**: besides the expense on the account you pay
  from, you now see the payment in the credit card's movements. Payments you had already made show
  up too.
- **Debts without a due date**: when you create a debt the due date starts empty and reads "No due
  date". When editing one you can remove its date.
- When you transfer a dollar statement to pesos, you now enter the date the bank charged it.

## Fixes

- A transferred statement showed US$0.00 and "Undo transfer" promised to give back 0. It now shows
  its real total.
- When you download the template with your data, a statement transferred to pesos imports again as
  transferred, without owing the dollars a second time.
- The template preview no longer fails on a dollar expense of an additional card with no dollar
  limit of its own. If the preview doesn't load, it says so and you can retry.
- The note on the template with your data reads on a single line.
- The mifik site shows up tidier in search engines.
