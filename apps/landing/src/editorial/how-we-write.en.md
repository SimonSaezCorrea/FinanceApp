_fik_'s guides explain how credit cards, accounts and statements work in Chile. Since they are about your money, here is how we make them: who writes them, where the facts come from, how often they are reviewed and what to do if you find a mistake.

## Who writes them

They are written and reviewed by **Simón Sáez**, founder of _fik_ ([about the author](/en/about/#author)). Every guide carries the author's name, the date it was published and the date it was last reviewed.

## Where the facts come from

- **From official rules and sources:** laws and decrees (Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional and the Diario Oficial), rules of Chile's financial market regulator (CMF, Comisión para el Mercado Financiero), the Central Bank of Chile, SERNAC (the consumer agency) and the SII (the tax service).
- **Every guide lists the sources it used at the end**, with links, so you can read the original.
- **The UF and dólar observado values** come from the Central Bank of Chile.
- **Examples use a generic bank** ("your bank") and made-up amounts to keep them clear. They don't describe any particular bank's terms: those are in your contract and your statement.

## How often they are reviewed

A guide is reviewed when a rule it covers changes and, even if nothing changes, at least once a year. Each guide's "Reviewed on" date says when that last happened.

## What they are not

They are general information, not financial or legal advice. To decide on your own case, check your contract or ask your bank.

## Independence

No bank or issuer pays to appear in a guide or for how it is described. _fik_ has no ads.

## How to report a mistake

If something is wrong or out of date, tell us:

- **By email**, at [contacto@mifik.cl](mailto:contacto@mifik.cl).
- **On GitHub**, by opening an issue in [_fik_'s repository](https://github.com/SimonSaezCorrea/FinanceApp/issues/new).

When we correct a guide, we update its review date.
